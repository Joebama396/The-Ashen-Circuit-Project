# The Ashen Circuit v1.10

## UI audio

- Adds the supplied Cursor Movement sound to menu navigation and menu entry.
- Adds the supplied Close Menu sound to menu, submenu, target, and manual exits.
- Adds the supplied Blocked Action sound to empty saves, unavailable combat
  commands, invalid item targets, and option controls that cannot move farther.
- Routes all three effects through the saved SFX volume setting.

## Sprite asset validation

- Normalizes the four recently imported Rian strips to their native 256x64
  runtime grid while preserving authored transparency and nearest-neighbor art.
